# Functional Description

> [!NOTE]  8.2 Sleep mode
> 
> The sleep mode is designed for low power consumption. If there is no REG_HIGH or
> overvoltage protection fault, **PCA9430 enters sleep mode when system MCU pulls
> SLEEP pin to HIGH.** If REG_HIGH or overvoltage protection fault occurs, PCA9430 will
> not enter sleep mode.
> 
> While in sleep mode, the system MCU can **wake up PCA9430 by driving SLEEP pin
> to LOW.** The device enters ECO_Mode and the system MCU configures PCA9430 for
> charging. If the REG_HIGH or overvoltage protection fault alerts in sleep mode, the
> device wakes up and enters ECO_Mode, so that the interrupt can be sent to system
> MCU to negotiate for lower power if necessary.

> [!NOTE] 8.7 VPWR LDO output for dead-battery
> 
> In dead battery mode, the WLC_L charging receiver requires the host AP or MCU to
> wake up first to setup NFC link and WLC wireless charging protocol. PCA9430 provides
> a LDO output by VPWR pin to supply host AP or MCU at dead battery condmons The
> maximum supply current is up to 50 mA, and the voltage can be adjusted by | ’c register
> according to Section 8.13.3.15.
> 
> When the WLC_L system wakes up from dead battery mode, the MCU power supply
> should be switched to system LDO from PMIC or dedicated LDO connected to battery.
> 
> The short circuit protection is integrated to protect the device. After short circuit protection
> is triggered, PCA9430 retries every 1 s.
> 
> In order to have system level flexibility, reverse current protection (RCP) circuit is
> integrated. This allows VPWR output to directly connect to an external LDO, with reverse
> current protection feature, to supply system MCU or AP.


> [!NOTE] 8.9 RXIR function
> 
> An RXIR function is integrated in PCA9430, providing an interrupt function for the
> wireless charger receiver side to wireless charger transmitter side. When the receiver
> side AP or MCU wants to notify the wireless charger transmitter side, the AP or MCU can
> configure the RXIR registers and trigger the RXIR. When the wireless charger transmitter
> side receives the RXIR request, the transmitter reads the NDEF in CRN120 chip of
> receiver side and responds to the request.



> [!NOTE] 8.10 ADC
> 
> The 10-bit ADC is integrated to measure the parameters for system control needs,
> enabling direct measurements of internal voltages, currents, on-chip temperatures, and
> off-chip temperatures (VRECT, IRECT, VOUT I0UT, chip temperature and NTC voltage).
> The measured results are stored in the 1°C register for host MCU or AP access.
> 
> The integrated multi-channel ADC can operate in three different modes: single-shot
> mode, continuous conversion mode, and burst mode. All three modes support an 8-
> sample averaging. The measured channel can be selected in the SAMPLES_EN register.
> 
> If averaging is enabled, the accumulator is cleared first, before the ADC samples a
> channel 8 times to collect fresh data. The average value is calculated from this set
> of values and stored in a 10-bit register which is used for ADC | 2c register value as
> described before.
> 
> In one shot mode, once the ADC value is available, the ADC_EN bit is cleared.
